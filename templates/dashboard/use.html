Setting Up the Dispenser Monitoring System

Follow these steps to successfully set up the dispenser monitoring system.

Set up device Connectivity

  1. Plug in and turn on the switch of the device.
  2. Open a browser and connect to the access point 192.168.4.1.
  3. Configure the wifi connection, enter the ssid and password.

Installing the device in the dispenser

  1. Attach the enclosure box on the right side of the dispenser.
  2. Place the sensor mount in the top side of the dispenser, right below the container.
